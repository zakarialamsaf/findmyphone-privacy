Privacy Policy — Find My Phone

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Contact: lamsafzakaria50@gmail.com

What this app does

Find My Phone lets phones that have been deliberately paired with each other ask one another

to report their location.

What we collect

  • Location. Precise or approximate location, collected only when a phone you have

paired with sends a locate request, and only at that moment. The app does not track you in

the background and does not build a location history.

  • A device identifier. An anonymous account id issued by Firebase Authentication and a

Firebase Cloud Messaging registration token, used to deliver locate requests to your phone.

  • A device name. Whatever you type in settings. Choose a nickname if you prefer.
  • A pairing code. A random six-character code that identifies your phone during pairing.
  • Advertising identifier. The app shows a single banner advert on its home screen, served

by Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's advertising ID to select and measure adverts.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts. The app does not

request access to your address book or your messages.

How it is used

Your location is used solely to deliver a locate request to the right phone and return the

answer to the phone that asked. Location is never used for advertising and is never sold.

The advertising ID is used only by Google AdMob to serve the banner on the home screen.

Who it is shared with

  • The devices you pair with. A paired device can request your location, and you can

request theirs. Pairing is mutual and either side can end it at any time.

  • Google Firebase, which hosts the authentication, database and messaging this app runs

on, as our data processor. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

  • Google AdMob, for the single banner advert, which receives your advertising ID but not

your location. See https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

  • OpenStreetMap, whose tile servers receive the map area you are viewing in order to serve

the map images.

Nobody else.

How long it is kept

  • Your device record and pairings are kept until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • A locate answer is kept in the request record; delete it from the app, or it is removed

with your device record.

Your control

  • See who can find you: Settings → Paired phones.
  • Revoke: tap any paired phone to unpair. It takes effect immediately.
  • Delete everything: open the app and choose Settings → Delete my data. This removes

your device record, your pairings and your last shared location from our service, and deletes

the anonymous account itself. You can also email the address above and we will delete your

data within 30 days.

  • Turn it off: revoke the location permission in Android settings, or uninstall.
  • Adverts: Settings → Ad privacy choices reopens the consent form where that applies, and

Android's own settings let you reset or delete your advertising ID at any time.

Transparency

Your phone shows a notification every time a paired device locates it. This is deliberate and

cannot be disabled from inside the app.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Database rules are default-deny: a device can read only its

own record, and a locate request is rejected by the server unless the two phones are paired.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and note the change in the

app's release notes.
